Green Card

Useful information about Green Card Insurance

The Green Card is an internationally recognised certificate that extends your motor third-party liability cover to member countries. Without it, you cannot legally take your vehicle across most borders in Europe and the region.

Cover is arranged by destination country and trip duration. The Green Card responds to damage you cause to third parties abroad, under the rules of the country where the accident happens.

We check which countries your trip requires, arrange the certificate quickly before departure and explain what the Green Card does and does not cover, so you are not caught out at the border or after a claim.

What is typically covered?

  • Third-party bodily injury caused abroad
  • Third-party vehicle and property damage caused abroad
  • Validity across the Green Card system member countries
  • Cover set by destination countries and travel dates
  • Handling under the local law of the country of accident

Who is it for?

Anyone driving their own vehicle outside Turkey — holidaymakers heading to Europe, professional drivers and transport operators crossing borders, and families on long road trips. The right country group and duration make all the difference.

What sets our agency apart

A Green Card is easy to overlook until you are at the border. We make it simple: we tell you exactly what you need for your route, issue it in time and remain reachable while you are away if anything goes wrong. Reliable service abroad starts with a clear conversation before you leave.
