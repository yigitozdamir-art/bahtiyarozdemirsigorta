Useful information about Property Insurance

Property Insurance focuses on the physical assets that keep a business running. Buildings, inventory, machinery, fixtures and commercial contents can be evaluated under a single risk approach.

The correct insurance value is essential. Building value, stock amount, fixtures, security measures and location details should be shared before the quotation is finalized.

After a loss, the first priority is to prevent further damage and document the incident. Photos, inventory records, invoices and repair estimates help the file progress more clearly.

Property Insurance is built around the physical assets on your balance sheet: buildings, machinery, fixtures, stock and electronic equipment. A well-structured policy combines fire and allied perils with theft, internal water damage and natural events, and can be extended with business interruption cover so that a single incident does not stop both your assets and your income at the same time.

What is typically covered?

  • Fire, lightning, explosion and smoke damage to buildings and contents
  • Theft and attempted theft of stock, fixtures and equipment
  • Internal water, storm, hail and weight of snow damage
  • Earthquake and volcanic eruption as an optional extension
  • Business interruption and loss of rent following an insured loss

Who is it for?

Manufacturers, warehouses, retail chains, office buildings, site managements and any organisation whose operations depend on physical premises and equipment. Accurate sums insured matter: undervaluation leads to proportional deductions at claim time, so we review valuations together before the policy starts.

What sets our agency apart

For clients with several properties and valuable assets the real problem is that policies are scattered across different dates and different insurers. Our agency brings the whole portfolio into a single overview, reminds you of renewal dates in advance and reviews sums insured against current values. This prevents both underinsurance and unnecessary duplicate cover. Most of our clients have been with us for years, and we take that continuity as the clearest evidence of satisfaction.
