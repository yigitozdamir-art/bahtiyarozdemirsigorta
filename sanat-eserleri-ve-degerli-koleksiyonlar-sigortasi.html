Useful information about Fine Art and Valuable Collections Insurance

Fine Art and Valuable Collections Insurance is suitable for galleries, collectors, museums and exhibition organizers. It protects items whose financial and emotional value requires a careful coverage approach.

A complete inventory, valuation documents, storage conditions and transport plans are essential before a quotation. The policy should reflect how and where the item is kept or displayed.

If damage occurs, the condition of the item should be documented immediately and expert opinions should be collected. Small physical damage can create significant value loss, so records are important.

Works of art carry values that ordinary property policies cannot express: authenticity, provenance and irreplaceability. Fine art insurance is therefore arranged on an agreed-value basis, ideally supported by appraisals, and follows the work "nail to nail" — from the wall it hangs on, through packing and transport, to the exhibition or the new owner. Cover can include restoration cost and the loss of value that remains even after a successful restoration.

What is typically covered?

  • Accidental damage, breakage, theft and mysterious disappearance
  • Transit cover during packing, shipping and installation
  • Exhibition and loan cover at galleries, fairs and museums
  • Restoration costs and depreciation after repair
  • Agreed-value settlement based on professional appraisal

Who is it for?

Private collectors, galleries, auction houses, museums, corporate collections and artists themselves. Documentation is the heart of this product: inventory lists, photographs, appraisals and provenance records both speed up claims and improve the terms insurers are willing to offer.

What sets our agency apart

The value of a collection is not always measured in figures; for its owner most pieces carry a story of their own. Our agency works with that sensitivity in mind and carries out the inventory and valuation process discreetly, without disturbing the security of the works. For periods such as exhibition, transit and storage we plan in advance so that cover continues without a gap. Protecting what is valuable begins with feeling that you are talking to someone who cares about it too.
